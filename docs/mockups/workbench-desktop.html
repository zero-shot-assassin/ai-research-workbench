How transformers learn

Explain how the transformer architecture changed natural language processing, and identify the key ideas that enabled modern large language models.
✦

Research assistant

The transformer marked a fundamental shift from processing language sequentially to understanding it through attention. Rather than reading one token at a time, it evaluates the relationships between every token in a sequence simultaneously.

The three ideas that changed the field

  1. 01

    Self-attention

    Each word dynamically weighs the relevance of every other word, capturing context across long passages without recurrent connections. 1

  2. 02

    Parallel computation

    Entire sequences can be processed at once. This made training dramatically faster and, crucially, made scaling to much larger datasets practical.
